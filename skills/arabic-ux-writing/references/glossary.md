# Glossary: English to Arabic

Default renderings for common interface, technology and organisational terms in Modern Standard Arabic. A product's own glossary always wins. Where two renderings are in real use, the first is the default and the note says when the other fits.

## Terms to handle with care

- **تحميل**: means both "loading" and, in older products, "download". Use «تنزيل» for download and keep «تحميل» for loading.
- **تحديث**: covers update, refresh and upgrade. Add an object: «تحديث التطبيق»، «تحديث الصفحة»; use «ترقية» for upgrade.
- **Dashboard**: «لوحة المعلومات» for viewing indicators, «لوحة التحكم» for administration. Do not use both for one screen.
- **Email**: «البريد الإلكتروني» for the address and the channel; «رسالة» for a single message. «إيميل» only in deliberately casual brands.
- **Online / Offline**: «متصل / غير متصل» for connection state; «عبر الإنترنت» for "online" as a channel; «حضوري» for in-person.
- **Free**: «مجاني» for price; «حر» for freedom; «متاح» for availability.
- **Please**: usually omit. See the style guide.
- **OK**: «حسنًا» or «موافق». Not «أوكي».
- **Share**: «مشاركة» for content; «حصة» or «سهم» in finance.
- **Regional variants**: mobile phone («جوال» in Saudi Arabia, «موبايل» or «محمول» in Egypt and the Levant, «نقّال» in parts of the Gulf and Maghreb); month names (Gregorian-derived in the Gulf and Egypt, Syriac-derived in the Levant and Iraq).
